## Watch rules (bind to every cron that touches jobs)
1. **Every hunt run (Part 1 + Part 2) MUST include at least 2 watchlist queries**: one broad (`site:linkedin.com/jobs OR site:builtinnyc.com <company> product OR product marketing OR APM 2027`), one targeted at any watchlist company with a known open program. Rotate through the list across the week if query budget is tight — but Google gets checked EVERY run.
2. **Immediate flag**: any match at a watchlist company goes to the TOP of the digest with a ⭐ prefix, regardless of overall rank, and is ALWAYS included even if the day's list is otherwise full (watchlist roles do not compete with the top-10 cutoff).
3. **Level flexibility**: for watchlist companies, the level-fit hard rule relaxes — new-grad AND 1–2yr-experience roles both qualify if they're PM/PMM/APM/growth/strategy (the candidate's 8-yr background makes mid-level PM roles plausible). Still flag non-sponsoring companies honestly.
4. **Never dedupe away a watchlist role's status change**: if a previously-tracked watchlist role closes or a new posting appears at the same company, note it in the digest.
5. **Backgrounder priority**: watchlist-company packages jump the queue (next backgrounder slot, regardless of order).
