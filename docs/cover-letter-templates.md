# Cover Letter Templates — three angles

Rules for any letter: under 300 words, no flattery, specific to company, humble but confident, never fabricate, end with clear availability. Pick the angle by role type.

## Angle 1 — PM roles ("strategist who builds")
Lead: 8 years turning consumer insight into strategy → now building the execution layer (Python, AI agents, automation). Bridge: product management IS translating user understanding into prioritized decisions, which is what I did for brands. Proof: full-funnel tracking (Bajaj), team leadership, $50M+ pitch wins. Close: what I'd learn + contribute at THIS company specifically.

## Angle 2 — PMM/Growth roles ("market maker")
Lead: 8 years deciding where brands show up and why — segmentation, media signals, CAC logic. Bridge: growth at [company] is the same discipline pointed at a single product. Proof: category breadth (CPG → fintech → automotive), pitch storytelling, analytics fluency. Close: tie to company's actual growth stage and market.

## Angle 3 — Strategy/AI-adjacent ("native to the shift")
Lead: I run AI agents as daily operating infrastructure (automated research systems, self-improving workflows) — not as a buzzword. Bridge: companies building AI products need people who understand both the tech's real capability ceiling and the buyer's psychology. Proof: portfolio projects, 8 years client-side judgment about what customers actually do vs say. Close: where I see [company]'s product meeting its market.

## Structure skeleton (all angles)
1. Hook: one sentence, specific to company or role. No "I am writing to apply."
2. Proof: 2–3 concrete outcomes with numbers where the master resume has them.
3. Bridge: why this pivot is an advantage, not a stretch.
4. Close: availability (full-time start May/June 2027), NYC-based, thank you.

## Anti-patterns (auto-reject in self-review)
- "I am excited to apply..." / "I am passionate about..." / "I believe I would be a great fit..." — no feelings-statements as openers; hook with something specific instead
- Em dashes and en dashes anywhere (the candidate's writing style excludes them — rewrite with commas, colons, or periods)
- Repeating the JD back as prose
- Any claim the master resume can't support
- Over 300 words without cause
